## Introduction

speechbubbleswithtext is a game navigation site: quick answers for trending game searches (codes, guides, release dates and more), each linked to the right site, plus a hand-picked directory of game websites. You can browse it without an account. This policy explains what we collect when you use the site and how we use it.

## Information We Collect

1. **Account information** — your name and email if you choose to sign up (for example, to submit a game site).
2. **Submissions** — the game name, link, category and description you submit to the directory.
3. **Usage data** — pages visited and outbound links clicked, used to keep answers and listings useful.
4. **Device information** — browser and operating system, used for compatibility.
5. **Cookies** — to remember your language and keep you signed in.

## How We Use It

We use this information to run and improve speechbubbleswithtext, publish and moderate game site submissions, respond to support tickets, and keep the service secure. We do not sell your personal information.

## Third-Party Sites

speechbubbleswithtext links to external game sites, wikis and stores. Their privacy practices are their own — please review them before sharing data.

## Your Rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data at any time by opening a support ticket.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest revision.
