## Introduction

Speech Bubbles is a free image editor that turns your ordinary photos into comics, memes, and stories — add speech bubbles, thought clouds, captions, and bold text, then download in seconds. You can start free without creating an account. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use Speech Bubbles.

## Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information to provide and improve the service:

1. **Account Information** — Your name and email address, provided when you choose to create an account. Registration is optional — you can use the editor and download watermarked images without one.
2. **Usage Data** — Information about how you interact with the service, such as features used and frequency of use.
3. **Device Information** — Device type, operating system, and browser type, used to optimize compatibility and performance.
4. **Cookies** — Small data files stored on your device to remember preferences and keep you signed in.
5. **Payment Information** — Billing details handled by trusted third-party payment processors. We do not store full card numbers on our servers.

## Images and Prompts You Submit

The core Speech Bubbles editor runs entirely in your browser. When you upload a photo to add speech bubbles, thought clouds, captions, or bold text, the image is processed locally on your device — it is not uploaded to or stored on our servers, and we do not use it to train any models. When you export, the only request made is a check of your account's entitlement (for example, whether HD, watermark-free downloads are included in your plan); no image data is sent with that request.

If you use the **AI generation** features, the content you submit is processed through our servers and our AI service provider:

- **Text-to-image:** Your text prompt is sent to our AI provider to generate an image.
- **Image-to-image (AI edit):** Your prompt together with your current image is uploaded and forwarded to our AI provider to produce an edited result.

We share this content with our AI provider solely to fulfill your request, and we do not use your images or prompts to train our own models.

## How We Use Your Information

We use the data we collect to:

- Provide, maintain, and improve the service.
- Process transactions and send related confirmations.
- Send technical notices, security alerts, and support messages.
- Respond to your questions, feedback, and support requests.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data only in these limited cases:

- With service providers who help us operate the service (including the AI provider that powers generation features), under confidentiality agreements.
- To comply with legal obligations or respond to lawful requests from public authorities.
- To protect our rights, property, or safety, or that of our users.

## Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encryption in transit, access controls, and regular reviews. No method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure, but we work continuously to protect your information.
