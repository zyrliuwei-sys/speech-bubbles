## Introduction

GameNav is a directory of online games. You can browse it without an account. This policy explains what we collect when you use the site and how we use it.

## Information We Collect

1. **Account information** — your name and email if you choose to sign up.
2. **Usage data** — pages visited and links clicked, used to improve rankings and the site.
3. **Device information** — browser and operating system, used for compatibility.
4. **Cookies** — to remember your language and keep you signed in.

## How We Use It

We use this information to run and improve GameNav, respond to submissions and support tickets, and keep the service secure. We do not sell your personal information.

## Third-Party Links

Game pages link to external sites and stores. Their privacy practices are their own — please review them before sharing data.

## Your Rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data at any time by opening a support ticket.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest revision.
