## List a game site for free

GameNav is a hand-picked directory — every site is reviewed by a person before it goes live. Listing is free.

## What we look for

1. **Playable today** — a live game, game platform, wiki, tool or community site.
2. **A working link** — the official site or store page players should land on.
3. **Honest info** — name, developer, genre, platforms and a one-line pitch.

## How to submit

Create an account, then open a support ticket from your dashboard with the details above. We usually reply within a few days.

## Featured placement

Want your site in the side rails or at the top of a category? Mention it in your ticket and we'll send you the options.
